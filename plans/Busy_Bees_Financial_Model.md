## 3. REVENUE PROJECTIONS

### Revenue Streams & Pricing

#### Daily Admissions
- Children 2+ years: $15
- Infants under 2: $5 (FREE with paid sibling)
- Average ticket: $13.50 (accounting for infant discounts)

#### Memberships & Packages
- Monthly Membership: $75/month
- 10-Visit Punch Card: $130 ($13/visit)
- Summer Special: $150 (3 months)

#### Birthday Parties
- Semi-Private Party: $350 (2 hours, 15 kids, +$12 additional)
- Private Party: $425 (2 hours, 15 kids, +$12 additional)
- Party Add-ons: $49-$125 each

#### Ancillary Revenue
- Snack Bar: 30% markup on cost
- Branded Socks: $8/pair (50% margin)
- Group Rates: $12/child (10+ children)

## 10. RISK FACTORS & MITIGATION

### Key Risks
1. **Seasonal Revenue Fluctuations** - Diversify with programs
2. **High Fixed Costs** - Negotiate flexible lease terms
3. **Competition** - Focus on unique value proposition
4. **Insurance Claims** - Comprehensive safety protocols
5. **Economic Downturn** - Recession-resistant pricing model

### Mitigation Strategies
- **Diversified Revenue**: Multiple income streams
- **Flexible Staffing**: Part-time employees for variable costs
- **Strong Marketing**: Community engagement and retention
- **Cash Management**: Maintain 3-month expense reserve
