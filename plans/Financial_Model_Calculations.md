## REVENUE CALCULATION METHODOLOGY

### Daily Admission Revenue Calculations

#### Conservative Scenario (Year 1)
```
Daily Visitors: 25 children average
Operating Days: 21 days per month (closed some Mondays)
Pricing Mix:
- 80% pay full price ($15) = 20 children
- 20% infants ($5) = 5 children (some free with siblings)
- Effective average ticket: $13.50

Monthly Calculation:
25 visitors/day × 21 days × $13.50 = $7,087.50
Add 33% for weekends/holidays = $9,450/month
Annual: $9,450 × 12 = $113,400
```

## BREAK-EVEN ANALYSIS CALCULATIONS

### Fixed Cost Break-Even
```
Monthly Fixed Costs: $10,735
Variable Cost per Child: $2.50 (supplies, processing)
Average Revenue per Child: $13.50
Contribution Margin: $13.50 - $2.50 = $11.00

Break-Even Volume:
Fixed Costs ÷ Contribution Margin = Break-Even Visitors
$10,735 ÷ $11.00 = 976 visitors per month
976 ÷ 21 days = 46 visitors per day (admission only)
```

### Total Break-Even (Including All Costs)
```
Total Monthly Costs: $24,110
Total Break-Even Revenue: $24,110
Daily Break-Even: $24,110 ÷ 21 = $1,148 per day

With Revenue Mix:
70% Admissions: $804 daily ($804 ÷ $13.50 = 60 visitors)
20% Memberships: $230 daily (allocated)
10% Parties/Other: $114 daily (allocated)
```
